Cold beet Soup

Description

A refreshing chilled soup made with roasted beets, yoghurt, cucumber, fresh herbs, and a hint of lemon for a light, vibrant flavour.

Ingredients

  • Beetroot
  • Cucumber
  • Greek yoghurt or sour cream
  • Buttermilk or kefir (optional)
  • Vegetable stock
  • Garlic
  • Lemon juice
  • Fresh dill
  • Chives or spring onions
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Boiled eggs (optional garnish)
  • Radish (optional)
  • Ice cubes (to chill)

Instructions

  1. Wash and boil the beetroots until tender (45–60 minutes).
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Peel and grate the beetroots.
  4. Dice the cucumber.
  5. Finely chop the dill and spring onions.
  6. Mix the yoghurt and vegetable stock until smooth.
  7. Add grated beetroot.
  8. Stir in cucumber, garlic, dill, and spring onions.
  9. Season with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  10. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  11. Garnish with sliced boiled eggs and more dill before serving.
