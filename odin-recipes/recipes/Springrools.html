Spring Rolls

Description

Crispy golden rolls filled with fresh vegetables and savoury seasonings, served with a sweet chilli dipping sauce for the perfect crunchy bite.

Ingredients

Filling

  • Spring roll wrappers
  • Olive oil
  • Carrot
  • Bean sprouts
  • Spring onions
  • Mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cornflour (optional)
  • Vegetable oil (for frying)

Seet Chilli Sauce

  • Sweet chilli sauce
  • Soy sauce (optional)
  • Lime juice (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat a little oil in a pan.
  2. Sauté garlic and ginger for about 30 seconds.
  3. Add mushrooms and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add cabbage and carrot.
  5. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes.
  6. Add bean sprouts and spring onions.
  7. Stir in soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and pepper.
  8. Cook for another minute.
  9. Let the filling cool completely.
  10. Place a spring roll wrapper in a diamond shape.
  11. Add 2 tablespoons of filling near the bottom corner.
  12. Fold the bottom corner over the filling.
  13. Fold in both side corners.
  14. Roll tightly toward the top corner.
  15. Seal the edge with a little water.
  16. Heat oil to 175°C (350°F).
  17. Fry the rolls for 3–4 minutes until golden brown.
  18. Drain on paper towels.
  19. Serve hot with sweet chilli sauce.
