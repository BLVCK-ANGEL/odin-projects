Roasted Pork

Description

Slow-roasted pork seasoned with aromatic herbs and spices, featuring a crisp, golden exterior and tender, flavourful meat

Ingredients

  • Pork loin, shoulder, or belly
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Rosemary
  • Thyme
  • Sage
  • Paprika
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Brown sugar (optional)
  • Dijon mustard (optional)
  • Apple cider vinegar or lemon juice
  • Chicken stock (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Pat the pork dry.
  3. Rub with olive oil.
  4. Mix garlic, rosemary, thyme, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  5. Coat the pork evenly with the seasoning.
  6. Place sliced onions in a roasting pan.
  7. Put the pork on top of the onions.
  8. Pour chicken stock into the pan.
  9. Roast for about 25–30 minutes per 500 g.
  10. Baste the pork every 30 minutes with the pan juices.
  11. Cook until the internal temperature reaches 63°C (145°F).
  12. Rest for 15 minutes.
  13. Slice and serve with the roasting juices.
