Salmon poke Bowls

Description

Fresh salmon cubes tossed with soy-sesame dressing, avocado, cucumber, edamame, and rice, topped with sesame seeds and spring onions.

Ingredients

  • Sushi-grade salmon
  • Olive oil
  • Avocado
  • Cucumber
  • Edamame
  • Carrot
  • Mango (optional)
  • Seaweed salad (optional)
  • Spring onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Nori strips

Poke Sauce

  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Rice vinegar
  • Honey
  • Sriracha (optional)
  • Fresh ginger
  • Garlic

Instructions

  1. Cook the sushi rice and allow it to cool.
  2. Dice the salmon into bite-sized cubes.
  3. Mix soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, honey, ginger, garlic, and sriracha.
  4. Toss the salmon gently in the sauce.
  5. Slice the avocado.
  6. Julienne the carrot.
  7. Slice the cucumber.
  8. Cook the edamame if frozen.
  9. Place rice in serving bowls.
  10. Arrange salmon, avocado, cucumber, carrot, and edamame over the rice.
  11. Sprinkle with sesame seeds, spring onions, and nori strips.
  12. Drizzle with any remaining sauce.
  13. Serve immediately.
