Grilled Chicken

Description

Tender chicken breast marinated in herbs and spices, flame-grilled to perfection and served juicy with a smoky, savoury finish.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast or thighs
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Lemon juice
  • Paprika
  • Oregano
  • Thyme
  • Rosemary
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Boiled eggs (optional garnish)
  • Fresh parsley (garnish)

Instructions

  1. Pound the chicken to an even thickness.
  2. Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, paprika, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat the chicken with the marinade.
  4. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Heat a grill or grill pan over medium-high heat.
  6. Grill for 6–7 minutes per side.
  7. Check that the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F).
  8. Brush with butter if desired.
  9. Rest for 5 minutes.
  10. Slice and garnish with parsley.
