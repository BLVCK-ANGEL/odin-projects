Chicken Taco

Description

Soft tortillas filled with seasoned grilled chicken, crisp lettuce, fresh tomatoes, cheddar cheese, and a zesty house salsa, finished with a drizzle of creamy sauce.

Ingredients

Chicken Filling

  • Boneless chicken breast or thighs
  • Olive oil
  • Onion powder
  • Garlic (minced)
  • Paprika
  • Cumin powder
  • Chilli powder
  • Oregano
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Lime juice

Taco Assembly

  • Soft flour or corn tortillas
  • Lettuce (shredded)
  • Tomato (diced)
  • Red onion (finely chopped)
  • Cheddar cheese (shredded)
  • Fresh cilantro/coriander
  • Sour cream (optional)
  • Salsa
  • Guacamole (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix olive oil, garlic, paprika, cumin, chilli powder, oregano, salt, pepper, and lime juice in a bowl.
  2. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the chicken for 5–7 minutes per side until fully cooked.
  5. Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes.
  6. Slice or shred the chicken.
  7. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for about 30 seconds on each side.
  8. Fill each tortilla with lettuce.
  9. Add the cooked chicken.
  10. Top with tomato, onion, cheese, salsa, and sour cream.
  11. Garnish with fresh coriander.
  12. Serve immediately.
